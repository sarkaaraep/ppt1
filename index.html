SCAN RESULTS

Physicochemical Properties

Class, thermal behavior, and solubility profile.

Class & Appearance

Class: substituted benzylamine / aromatic amine derivative. Appearance: white to off‑white crystalline powder.

Melting & Solubility

Melting point: free base ~233–234°C, HCl salt 233–235°C. Sparingly soluble in cold water; soluble in methanol & ethanol.

Lipophilicity & pH

Log P ~2.3–3.0 (moderately lipophilic). pH 4.0–5.5, density ~1.59 g/cm³.

AMBROXOL DYNAMICS

Mechanism of Action

Three coordinated effects on the airway.

01

Secretolytic & Mucolytic

Depolymerizes acidic mucopolysaccharide fibers in bronchial secretions, directly reducing viscosity and fluidifying mucus.

02

Surfactant Stimulation

Stimulates Type II pneumocytes to synthesize and release surfactant, reducing alveolar surface tension and preventing collapse.

03

Ciliary & Analgesic Action

Restores ciliary beat frequency for rapid mucociliary clearance; inhibits voltage‑gated Na⁺ channels, relieving sore throat pain.

PHARMACOKINETIC PROFILE

ADME

Absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion.

A

Absorption

Rapidly and almost completely absorbed post oral administration. First‑pass hepatic metabolism reduces bioavailability to ~70%.

D

Distribution

Extensive tissue distribution with high affinity for pulmonary tissue. High plasma protein binding (~90%), primarily to albumin.

M

Metabolism

Extensively metabolized in the liver via CYP3A4 oxidation and glucuronidation into dibromoanthranilic acid and glucuronides.

E

Excretion

Eliminated primarily via renal clearance (~80%) as water‑soluble glucuronide metabolites and unchanged drug. T½ ~10 hours.

APPLIED USE

Clinical Indications & Applications

Where Ambroxol earns its place in therapy.

Acute & Chronic Bronchitis

Facilitates mucus expulsion, decreases cough frequency, reduces airway hyper‑reactivity, and improves bronchopulmonary patency.

COPD & Bronchiectasis

Significantly reduces acute exacerbation frequency and severity in COPD and bronchiectatic conditions.

Infant Respiratory Distress (IRDS)

Administered perinatally or neonatally to stimulate endogenous surfactant production in pre‑term infants with IRDS.

RISK ASSESSMENT

Safety Profile & Contraindications

Precautions, restrictions, and tolerability.

⚠ PRECAUTIONS & RESTRICTIONS
⚠

Known Allergy

Contraindicated in patients with hypersensitivity to Ambroxol or Bromhexine.

⚠

Peptic Ulcer Disease

Use with extreme caution in active gastric or duodenal ulceration.

⚠

Pregnancy

Avoid usage during the first trimester of pregnancy.

● TOLERABILITY & SIDE EFFECTS
●

Gastrointestinal

Mild pyrosis, dyspepsia, nausea, and occasional dysgeusia (altered taste).

●

Hypersensitivity

Rare cutaneous reactions, urticaria, or severe anaphylactic reactions.
